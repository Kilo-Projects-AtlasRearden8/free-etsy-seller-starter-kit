# Free Etsy Seller Starter Kit

Fillable, printable, no account needed. Credit **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.

A no-cost starter kit for new Etsy sellers: shop policies, listing worksheet,
pricing calculator, packaging slip, and income log. Every page is designed to
be filled in on screen or printed by hand — no signup, no tracking, nothing to
buy.

## Who this is for

You're opening (or thinking about opening) an Etsy shop and you want a simple
place to organize your policies, plan your listings, price for profit, pack
orders, and track income — without creating another account or paying for a
tool. This kit is built for beginners: plain language, step by step, nothing
condescending. Print what you need, fill in the rest on screen, and keep
everything on your own computer.

## What's inside

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `shop-policies.md` / `shop-policies.html` | Fill-in shop policies: processing times, shipping, returns, custom orders, FAQ |
| `listing-worksheet.md` / `listing-worksheet.html` | Prep a listing before you publish: title, photos, details, pricing, tags, description |
| `pricing-calculator.md` / `pricing-calculator.html` | Work out materials, labor, Etsy fees, shipping, and a profitable price (totals calculate live in your browser) |
| `packaging-slip.md` / `packaging-slip.html` | Print-and-fill slip to tuck into every order |
| `income-log.md` / `income-log.html` | Track sales, fees, and net income by month |

## How to use

1. Open the folder and double-click any `.html` file. It opens in your web
   browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
2. Fill in the fields on screen, or print the page and write by hand.
3. To print: press **Ctrl+P** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+P** (Mac). Each page has
   print styles that hide form borders and controls so your printout looks clean.
4. Save a copy for your records. Nothing is sent anywhere — everything stays
   on your computer.
